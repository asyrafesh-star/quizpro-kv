Outfits - Fantasy (Standard). Downloaded by the user from the creator's itch.io.

This prototype uses one male base character, four original hair meshes and
six transformed variants, two upper-body cuts in ten preset colour choices,
one fixed pair of trousers and one shoe mesh in three colour choices.
It does not contain ten independently authored clothing models or shoe meshes.

Changes: reduced textures to at most 512px; omitted normal/ORM maps from active
materials; removed hidden base-body triangles to avoid clipping; combined
head, upper clothing and fixed lower clothing; procedural bone motion.

Three.js addons: GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and BufferGeometryUtils, r181,
